Everything further down is derived from the same registry — set a provider's presence or its capabilities on the left and watch the roster, the stored fact, and the four consumers all move together. The question this page exists to answer: when Claude goes dark, does the right stuff disappear everywhere at once? If any surface keeps offering something no longer possible, the shape is wrong.
