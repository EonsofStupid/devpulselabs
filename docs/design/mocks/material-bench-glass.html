GLASS — three stops on one scale

One material, three configured presets that define a scale; the slider below shows the values in between. Every colour is the real ColdLight OKLCH engine, tone-relative — change the tone and every stop re-derives. Default is the operator's preferred baseline: hard elevation, crisp edge, no glow. Nothing here uses blur (doctrine law 6): the "glass" read comes from fill alpha + a painted sheen band.

3 · Why this is not today's glass

Today tokens/matrix.json gives glass three keys — fill, ink, edge_color — which makes it byte-identical to metallic. The props above are what a material needs to actually be that material.
