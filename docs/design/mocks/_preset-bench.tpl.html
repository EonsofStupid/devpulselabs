Every lineage, three ways

A preset is a named look within a lineage — a legal coordinate on the matrix, never a free-hand style. This page is generated from the authored tokens (matrix.json + hues.json): the OKLCH ladders are computed with the real ColdLight curves, and the generator refuses to emit a coordinate its ancestor forbids, or a set of three where two resolve to the same material. The shell is identical everywhere, so only the formula differs.

What building this exposed in the authored data — and what was done

  • Vibron is a coordinate now. The operator ruled brutalist · massive · plate · hard-cast = Vibron, and that it needed one new material. traits.json even recorded the resolution in its blurb — but plate was never added, so Vibron existed only as a trait in the parallel system. It decomposes exactly: radius 6 + edge 2.0 is massive, shadow [3,3] blur 0 black@230 is hard-cast, and fill OVERLAY + ink BASE + edge HAIRLINE is plate. The material is authored and ruled for all eight lineages.
  • bloom shipped a blur — fixed. Aero's native atmosphere authored blur: 12/16, violating doctrine law 6 (“No blur, ever”) and matrix §11.4's own ruling that aero is interpreted no-blur. Re-authored as a painted ring falloff — concentric hard rings at falling alpha — plus two hard directional shadows. Aero reads soft here without one blurred pixel.
  • The gloss band was invisible — fixed. sheen and bloom painted their specular band as OVERLAY @ 90: a dark surface on a dark fill, a measured 4/255 delta. A specular band must be lit; both now use ink STRONG.
  • A third of the material axis was duplicate data — fixed. matte ≡ raw, glass ≡ metallic and paper ≡ clay were byte-identical in fill, ink and edge: 9 materials that painted 6, which capped bauhaus, swiss and constructivist at two distinct materials each. 10 materials now paint 10. raw and paper kept their fills because they carry ratified looks (neo, and the swiss + editorial natives); the duplicates moved instead — matte went deep and absorbing, metallic became dark metal with a lit rim, and clay became a RECESSED well, because the doctrine says content sits in clay.
