6 · What ratifying this mock means

  • Already law: neubrutalist, claymorphic, cyber, retro are trait DATA (tokens/traits.json) — the mixes above are compose() doing what it already does. No new machinery.
  • NEW — liquid glass: one added base trait (translucent fill + specular edge + sheen tint). Needs a small vocabulary addition: a specular-edge axis or a sheen element. Authored as data, regenerated — never hand-rolled.
  • NEW — the icon language: Lucide sources → generated stroke registry (ADR-015, plan icon-language-v3a.md). Styled at PAINT time (brutal offset-dup, clay/glass chips) — assets stay pure geometry, so your own SVGs later inherit every treatment for free.
  • Doctrine holds: zero real blur anywhere on this page — glows are ring falloffs, glass is alpha + gradients, scanlines are painted lines. Everything here is first-frame cheap in egui.
  • Translated from your references (docs/design/references/): clay goes DEEPER — rim light, heavier extrusion, NEON INK (painted multi-copy bloom, never a blur pass) — the pills and rail option B; neubrutalist gains the comic energy — bevel slabs, halftone print texture, starburst glow (all painted); the metal machine-console look (claymorphism-cyber.png) is a candidate flavor for the Present / show-machine surface, its own later pass; the two editor shots map to flat wells + liquid-glass/cyber chrome + glow spotlights (WiredFRONT, Guide Workbench).
  • RULED (operator, 2026-07-30): clay IS a button. The "clay is never a button" line was the port's inference, never operator law — retired in doctrine.md. Both bases are actionable: brutal presses ONTO its shadow, clay sinks INTO its extrusion, neon ink carries clay's tappability. Clay hover/active state data lands in traits.json with the trait pass.
  • Sequence after ratification: V3a (registry + variants, no UI) → V3b+V4 (the rail wears icons as clear buttons) → liquid-glass trait pass → V5 Home showcase composes all of it.
