DevPULSE Labs · Workbench Editor · preset proposal

Four preset sets, one rule: pick one, and there are only 4–7 things left to touch.

Every preset below is a named bundle of refs into the registries that already exist — 13 fonts, 10 moods, the fluid ramp, the motion effects. Zero new styling code. What is new is the exposes list: the traits a preset hands you. Everything not in that list is decided for you and cannot be reached from the overlay at all.

4 ≤ exposes ≤ 7 — or the build fails
colour always exposed — or the build fails
only knobs its entity has — a text preset exposing ambience fails the build
What is real here and what is not. Every colour on this page is computed from hues.json and moods.json through the real OKLCH ramp — the gradients are the actual moods. The typefaces are not: the 13 real faces ship as TTFs in the repo and cannot be loaded here, so type specimens use a stand-in and the true face is named as data on every tile. Judge the colour and the knob sets; take the letterforms on trust until it is on the exe.
